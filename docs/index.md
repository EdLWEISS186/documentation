# Introduction

**DAC Labs** is a technology-driven research and development organization focused on advanced distributed systems, with a specific emphasis on blockchain architectures designed for long-term scalability, security, and interoperability. Out team operates at the intersection of blockchain engineering, applied cryptography, and emerging quantum technologies.

The **DAC Blockchain** is the core infrastructure developed by DAC Labs. It is designed as a next-generation distributed ledger that goes beyond traditional transactional use cases, enabling programmable value, advanced governance models, and native support for complex digital assets and services. The architecture prioritizes modularity, performance, and future-proof design choices, allowing the protocol to evolve alongside technological and regulatory landscapes.

The network is named **DAC Quantum Chain** to reflect both its architectural philosophy and its long-term vision. “Quantum” does not merely reference future compatibility with post-quantum cryptography, but also symbolizes a paradigm shift in how trust, computation, and value exchange are structured within the network. DAC Quantum Chain is built to accommodate quantum-resilient principles and to integrate, when appropriate, cryptographic standards designed to withstand advances in quantum computing.

This documentation provides a technical and conceptual overview of the DAC ecosystem, its components, and its design rationale.

_Further updates and extensions of this documentation will be released in conjunction with the publication of the DAC Quantum Chain mainnet node, ensuring alignment between protocol implementation and official technical specifications._
