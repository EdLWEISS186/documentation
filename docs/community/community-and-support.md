# Community and Support

**DAC Blockchain** is an open project built by and for the community. We believe in the power of open-source collaboration and welcome contributions from developers, enthusiasts, and partners around the world.

Whether you are looking to run a node, build a decentralized application (dApp), or simply learn more about the technology, our community channels are the best place to get started.

### Join the Conversation <a href="#join-the-conversation" id="join-the-conversation"></a>

Stay updated with the latest announcements, participate in technical discussions, and direct your questions to the team and other community members on our official social channels:

* **Telegram**: [Join our Telegram Group](https://t.me/dac_chain) _Real-time discussions, support, and community announcements._
* **Discord**: [Join our Discord Server](https://discord.gg/dacchain) _Developer community, technical support, and discussions._
* **X (formerly Twitter)**: [Follow us on X](https://x.com/dac_chain) _Latest news, updates, and ecosystem highlights._
* **LinkedIn**: [Connect on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dac-labs/) _Professional network, partnerships, and general updates._
* **Official Website**: [Visit our website](https://dachain.tech/) _General updates, documentation, and community events._

### Contributing to DAC <a href="#contributing-to-dac" id="contributing-to-dac"></a>

We actively encourage contributions to the DAC ecosystem. If you are interested in contributing code, documentation, or feedback, please check our repositories and join our developer discussions on Telegram or X.

Together, we are building a more decentralized future.
